Rejection Sampling Bench

Draw from the draft q, accept with probability min(1, p/q), and on rejection draw from the residual norm(max(0, p−q)). The output is distributed exactly as p. No neural network involved.

Broken mode. On rejection this resamples from p instead of the residual. Watch the acceptance rate below — it does not move. Only the distribution does.
